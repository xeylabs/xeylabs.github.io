RULES.

R.01 — BUILD IN PUBLIC.

Everything the lab makes gets logged in The Log. Work that isn't written down didn't happen.

R.02 — RECEIPTS, NOT ROADMAPS.

No decks, no fake progress, no theater. Status labels on the project index are the source of truth: shipping / queued / draft.

R.03 — PRIVACY IS THE DEFAULT.

No cookies, no tracking, no analytics. If that ever changes, the privacy page changes first, in plain language, before the feature ships.

R.04 — OPEN CODE, HONEST STATUS.

Published code is MIT licensed. Some research stays private while it's fragile — when it ships, it ships with a log entry.

R.05 — ONE EXPERIMENT, ONE ENTRY.

The Log is receipts, not filler. We don't post to fill a calendar.

R.06 — SAY IT PLAIN.

Plain language, short sentences. If a sentence needs a footnote to sound honest, it gets rewritten.

These rules change when the lab learns. Every amendment is public — what changed and why goes in The Log.
