Manifesto

FIELD NOTES
XEY/DOCTRINE

We'd rather ship an honest prototype this month than a perfect slide deck next year. If it can't be shared, it isn't done. We pick durable, boring technology over whatever is trending.

P.01

Small by design

A tiny team keeps decisions fast and hands-on. Everyone builds.

P.02

Build in the open

Public by default. The repo is the record of what happened.

P.03

Craft over hype

Curiosity is the roadmap. The work has to outlive the trend.
