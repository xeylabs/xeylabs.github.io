What we do

Three tracks, one lab

Everything we do falls into one of three loops — explore, share, build.

Applied research

Exploring new tools, protocols, and interfaces through small, fast experiments — learn by building, not by guessing.

Open source

Building in the open. Most of what we learn ends up public on GitHub, free for anyone to use and improve.

Product prototypes

Turning the experiments that stick into small, useful products with real users and real feedback loops.
